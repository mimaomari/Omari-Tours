Toyota Rumion TX

Executive 7-Seater MPV

The Toyota Rumion TX is our flagship executive multi-purpose vehicle, purpose-built for discerning international tourists, executive travelers, and visiting families. Combining modern Japanese refinement with spacious 7-passenger capability, it features individual rear-zone climate control, plush ergonomic seating, elevated scenic windows, and a quiet, insulated cabin that glides effortlessly across Cape Town’s mountain passes, coastal highways, and city boulevards. Whether configured as an expansive 6-passenger sightseeing tourer or flat-folded to accommodate up to 4 travelers with full airport luggage sets, it ensures every journey is safe, tranquil, and distinctly first-class.
